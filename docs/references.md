The survey is a narrative review and a non-peer-reviewed preprint; its main search cutoff is 12 September 2026, with a historical supplement dated 20 September 2026.

**Coverage note:** The supplied PDF numbers its bibliography from 1 to 405, although Section 2.2 reports 404 distinct works. This archive preserves all **405 numbered entries** and the original numbering. No duplicate source identifiers were found during import.

**Provenance:** Every source link below matches an embedded hyperlink in the supplied PDF. Titles, author strings, years, and publication descriptions are transcribed from that bibliography, with line wrapping and obvious extraction artifacts repaired. This is an archive, not an independent verification of all metadata, a full-text review of all papers, or evidence that every listed method establishes RSI. Use the curated README for selected, checked reading paths.

Original author lists may be abbreviated with “et al.”. The [JSON archive](../data/references.json) retains raw extracted citation text, source URLs, and per-entry provenance.
